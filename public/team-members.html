Service areas: Southern California

Eric Lin is the owner and founder of LineWork Development, a San Marino–based residential design-build firm serving Greater Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, and Orange County. His work centers on bringing architectural intent, construction execution, and homeowner-focused project leadership together in one accountable process. Professional bio: Eric Lin is the Owner and founder of LineWork Development, a Southern California residential design-build firm specializing in thoughtfully planned home transformations. Based in San Marino, Lin leads a team focused on bridging the common gap between architectural design and construction reality—giving homeowners strategic direction, technical expertise, and a more coordinated path from early concept through final completion. Lin’s approach is grounded in the idea that a contractor should serve as a trusted homeowner advocate, not simply as a builder. Through LineWork Development, he brings together architects, project managers, and skilled tradespeople under a single, integrated delivery model designed to improve communication, maintain budget and schedule visibility, and preserve the original design vision throughout the build. His work emphasizes sophisticated residential additions, seamless “invisible transitions” between existing and new spaces, interior modernizations, kitchen remodels, full-home renovations, custom accessory dwelling units, and ground-up residential construction. The company also provides design coordination, architectural drawings, space planning, custom cabinetry, historic-building conservation, green-building work, and project management for projects ranging from targeted remodels to custom homes. Under Lin’s leadership, LineWork Development uses a transparent, digital project-management approach that gives clients real-time access to budget tracking, schedule updates, project documents, photographs, and decisions. This focus on clarity and single-point accountability reflects his commitment to making complex residential construction more organized, predictable, and client-centered. LineWork Development serves established neighborhoods throughout Southern California, including San Marino, Pasadena, South Pasadena, San Gabriel, La Cañada Flintridge, Beverly Hills, Brentwood, Santa Monica, Culver City, Sherman Oaks, Calabasas, Burbank, Glendale, Hollywood, and West Hollywood.
